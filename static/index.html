Shelf Scout

Which of these books are worth buying?

Photograph a shelf. You'll get the books worth pulling, what each one resells for, and the most you should pay.

  • Stand square to the shelf and fill the frame with one or two rows of spines.
  • Tilt the phone a little to kill glare on glossy jackets.
  • Pull the books marked Pull, then type the ISBN of anything marked Look closer.
